JOB QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a Dreypella account to apply?

Yes. Create an account so your application and verification information can be associated with you.

Can students apply?

Many Dreypella opportunities are designed with students and flexible schedules in mind. Eligibility depends on the specific opportunity.

Do delivery roles require verification?

Yes. Delivery and driving roles may require identity, document and other verification before approval.

Are all opportunities available in every location?

No. Availability depends on the location, current platform operations and the requirements of each role.
