IMPORTANT

Before you start.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create a Dreypella account before applying for an earning opportunity.

Is every earning opportunity available to everyone?

No. Some opportunities require specific equipment, verification, location, experience or approval.

How do I receive my earnings?

Eligible earnings are handled through the Dreypella account and wallet system according to the applicable role and platform rules.

Can I choose more than one opportunity?

Depending on eligibility and platform requirements, you may be able to participate in multiple opportunities.
