DREYPELLA RIDE

Privacy Policy

How Dreypella Ride handles your information

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Dreypella Ride respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information provided through the Dreypella Ride platform, website, applications and services.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected, how that information may be used, how it may be protected and the circumstances in which it may be shared.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, Dreypella Ride may collect information such as:

  • Full name and account details.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Account type and role.
  • Referral information.
  • Identity and verification information.
  • Student or partner verification documents where required.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Wallet and earnings information.
  • Ride, delivery and booking information.
  • Pickup and delivery locations.
  • Device and technical information.
  • Location information when location services are used.

3. Account Information

Information provided during registration is used to create and manage your Dreypella Ride account.

You are responsible for ensuring that information supplied to Dreypella Ride is accurate and up to date.

4. Location and GPS Information

Certain Dreypella Ride services may require location information to provide features such as route planning, distance calculation, estimated arrival times, pickup, delivery and live tracking.

Location access may depend on the permissions you grant through your device or browser.

Where live tracking is used, location information may be processed while the relevant ride or delivery service is active.

5. Verification Information

Some account types may require additional verification. This may include identity, student, guarantor, vehicle, driver, rider, walker, vendor or other partner documents.

Verification information is used to help establish eligibility, protect the platform, reduce fraud and support safe operations.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Process rides and deliveries.
  • Calculate routes, distance and estimated arrival times.
  • Process payments and wallet transactions.
  • Process partner earnings and withdrawals.
  • Provide customer and platform support.
  • Verify users and partners.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Improve platform functionality and services.
  • Communicate important service information.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Payments and Financial Information

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers or financial service providers.

Dreypella Ride may retain transaction records necessary for account management, reconciliation, refunds, earnings, wallet balances, withdrawals, dispute resolution and legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Sharing Information

Dreypella Ride may share information when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, process transactions, complete verification, operate the platform, prevent fraud, provide support or comply with legal requirements.

Depending on the service, relevant information may be made available to customers, delivery partners, drivers, riders, walkers, vendors, ambassadors, payment providers, technology providers or other authorized service providers.

Dreypella Ride does not intend to sell personal information as a commercial product.

9. Third-Party Services

Dreypella Ride may use third-party technologies and service providers to support authentication, payments, maps, location services, hosting, communications, analytics, security and other platform functions.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices as applicable.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Dreypella Ride may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support login sessions, preferences, security and platform functionality.

11. Account Security

Dreypella Ride uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, complete transactions, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate business interests.

13. Your Responsibilities

You should protect your account credentials and should notify Dreypella Ride if you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization.

14. Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law and legitimate operational requirements, you may request information concerning your personal data or ask for correction of inaccurate information.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, transaction or fraud-prevention purposes.

15. Children's Privacy

Dreypella Ride services are not intended to be used by individuals who are not legally permitted to use the relevant service.

Where age or eligibility restrictions apply, users are expected to comply with those requirements.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Dreypella Ride may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes may be published on the platform and may include an updated version number and effective date.

17. Contact

Questions or privacy-related requests should be directed to Dreypella Ride through its official contact or support channels.
