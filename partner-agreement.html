DREYPELLA RIDE

Partner Agreement

Dreypella Ride Partner Terms

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

This Partner Agreement applies to individuals or businesses who register to participate in Dreypella Ride partner services, including Campus Ambassadors, Vendors, Delivery Walkers, Delivery Riders and Drivers.

1. Acceptance of the Agreement

By registering as a partner and accepting this agreement, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to comply with this Partner Agreement, the Dreypella Ride Terms & Conditions and the Dreypella Ride Privacy Policy.

Acceptance of this agreement creates a record that you agreed to the applicable partner terms and version in effect at the time of registration.

2. Partner Eligibility

Partners must provide accurate information and satisfy the eligibility and verification requirements applicable to their selected role.

Dreypella Ride may request additional information or documents before allowing a partner to perform services or receive certain platform benefits.

3. Partner Verification

Depending on the partner role, verification may include identity information, student information, guarantor information, vehicle information, driver's documentation, business information or other supporting documents.

Providing false, altered or misleading information may result in rejection, suspension or termination of the partner account.

4. Independent Partner Relationship

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, registration as a partner does not by itself create an employment relationship between the partner and Dreypella Ride.

Partners are responsible for complying with applicable laws, licences, permits, taxes and professional requirements relating to their activities.

5. Partner Responsibilities

Partners agree to:

  • Provide truthful and accurate information.
  • Complete required verification.
  • Perform accepted assignments responsibly.
  • Treat customers and other partners respectfully.
  • Protect customer and platform information.
  • Follow safety requirements and applicable laws.
  • Maintain appropriate communication with customers.
  • Report incidents, disputes or problems promptly.
  • Use the platform only for legitimate purposes.

6. Delivery Walkers

Delivery Walkers may accept eligible delivery assignments and are responsible for safely collecting, transporting and delivering accepted packages.

Walkers must not intentionally delay, tamper with, open, damage or improperly handle a customer's package.

7. Riders and Drivers

Riders and Drivers must comply with applicable transportation, road safety, vehicle and licensing requirements.

They are responsible for operating their vehicles safely and maintaining any documentation required for their role.

A partner must not knowingly accept an assignment that they are not legally or operationally qualified to perform.

8. Vendors

Vendors are responsible for the products, services, prices, descriptions, availability and fulfilment of items listed through the marketplace.

Vendors must not list illegal, counterfeit, dangerous or prohibited products.

Vendors must provide customers with products or services that reasonably correspond to the information displayed on the platform.

9. Campus Ambassadors

Campus Ambassadors may promote Dreypella Ride and refer eligible customers, vendors or partners in accordance with approved Dreypella Ride programmes.

Ambassadors must not make false promises, misrepresent Dreypella Ride, manipulate referrals or use deceptive marketing practices.

10. Assignments and Acceptance

A partner may receive or accept assignments through available Dreypella Ride systems.

Once an assignment has been accepted, the partner is expected to make reasonable efforts to complete it unless circumstances outside the partner's reasonable control prevent completion.

11. Earnings and Platform Revenue

Partner earnings are calculated according to the pricing, revenue-sharing, commission and incentive rules applicable to the relevant service or partner programme.

Dreypella Ride may display earnings, transaction records, commissions, fees and other financial information through the partner's account or wallet.

Dreypella Ride may update pricing and partner earning structures where operational or business changes require it, subject to applicable terms and notices.

12. Wallets and Withdrawals

Eligible partners may receive earnings through a Dreypella Ride wallet or another supported payment method.

Withdrawals may be subject to verification, transaction processing, applicable limits, review and administrative approval.

Dreypella Ride may delay or restrict a withdrawal where there is a legitimate security, fraud, dispute, verification or transaction-reconciliation concern.

13. Customer Payments

Partners must not unlawfully collect, divert, manipulate or retain customer payments that are required to be processed through Dreypella Ride's approved payment systems.

Partners must not falsely report completed trips, deliveries, orders or transactions for the purpose of obtaining earnings or incentives.

14. Prohibited Conduct

Partners must not:

  • Commit fraud or manipulate platform transactions.
  • Create fraudulent accounts or referrals.
  • Impersonate another person.
  • Share account credentials with unauthorized persons.
  • Manipulate GPS, location or assignment information.
  • Abuse customers or other partners.
  • Steal, tamper with or misuse customer property.
  • Attempt to bypass platform security.
  • Use customer information for unauthorized purposes.
  • Engage in unlawful activities through the platform.

15. Customer and Data Confidentiality

Partners may receive personal or operational information about customers, recipients, vendors, trips or deliveries.

Such information must only be used for legitimate Dreypella Ride services and must not be copied, sold, disclosed or used for unrelated purposes.

16. Safety and Incident Reporting

Partners must prioritize reasonable safety during assignments.

Accidents, lost packages, suspected fraud, customer disputes, security incidents or other serious problems should be reported to Dreypella Ride through the appropriate support channel as soon as reasonably possible.

17. Platform Monitoring

Dreypella Ride may monitor platform activity, transaction records, assignment history, location information where applicable and other operational information for safety, service quality, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and platform administration.

18. Suspension and Termination

Dreypella Ride may temporarily restrict or suspend a partner account where necessary to investigate suspected fraud, misconduct, safety concerns, verification issues, policy violations or other legitimate operational concerns.

A partner relationship may be terminated where the partner violates applicable agreements, fails required verification, engages in serious misconduct or otherwise becomes ineligible to participate.

19. Outstanding Transactions

Termination or suspension does not automatically erase valid transaction records, financial obligations, customer claims, dispute records or other responsibilities that arose before suspension or termination.

20. Changes to the Partner Agreement

Dreypella Ride may update this Partner Agreement from time to time.

Updated agreements may receive a new version number and effective date. Where required, partners may be asked to review and accept an updated agreement before continuing certain partner activities.

21. Agreement Records

Dreypella Ride may maintain records showing the partner's acceptance of this agreement, including the agreement version, partner role and acceptance date.

22. Relationship With Other Terms

This Partner Agreement should be read together with the Dreypella Ride Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Additional programme-specific rules may apply to individual partner roles or services.

23. Contact and Support

Partners should use Dreypella Ride's official support and contact channels for questions, disputes, incidents, account issues or clarification regarding partner activities.
