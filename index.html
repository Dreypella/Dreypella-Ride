FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Dreypella Ride.

What is Dreypella Ride?

Dreypella Ride is a student-focused mobility, logistics, marketplace and earning platform.

Who can use Dreypella Ride?

Students, customers, vendors, delivery partners, drivers and other verified users can use available services.

Do I need an account?

Yes. An account is required for protected services such as bookings, deliveries, orders and wallet features.

Can I track my ride or delivery?

Where live tracking is available, customers can follow their active ride or delivery.

How do I pay?

Available payment methods are shown during the relevant booking, delivery or checkout process.

How can I earn with Dreypella?

Explore opportunities such as delivery, driving, campus ambassador, marketing and support roles.

Can I join as a vendor?

Yes. Eligible businesses and student vendors can apply to join the Dreypella marketplace.

Where is Dreypella available?

Dreypella is starting with the LAUTECH and Ogbomoso community and will expand as the platform grows.

How do I contact support?

Visit our support or contact page for help with your account, booking, delivery or order.
